# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises. The roles below are adaptable: on smaller projects, one person may cover multiple roles, or a role may be shared across people. Regardless of team structure, assign each responsibility and decision clearly.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## UX/UI Designers and User Researchers

### Role Summary
UX/UI Designers and User Researchers investigate user needs and shape accessible, usable experiences that address those needs.

### Responsibilities
- Plan and conduct user research or usability testing appropriate to the project
- Create user journeys, flows, wireframes, prototypes, and interaction or visual design specifications
- Identify accessibility and usability considerations and document design decisions
- Share research findings and validate proposed solutions with users

### Key Deliverables and Coordination
- Produce research findings, experience designs, and usability or accessibility acceptance considerations
- Partner with the Product Manager to connect user needs to product outcomes and priorities
- Work with Developers and the Technical Lead on feasibility and implementation trade-offs
- Coordinate with QA/Testing to include usability and accessibility scenarios in validation
- Share findings with Stakeholders and the Project Manager so decisions, dependencies, and timing are visible

## Technical Leads and Architects

### Role Summary
Technical Leads and Architects guide technical direction and help ensure that the solution is secure, maintainable, and compatible with the project's systems and constraints.

### Responsibilities
- Guide technical design, architecture, and integration decisions
- Identify technical risks, constraints, and dependencies; recommend mitigations
- Support Developers through implementation planning and technical reviews
- Document significant technical decisions and communicate trade-offs

### Key Deliverables and Coordination
- Maintain or contribute to design documents, architecture decisions, and technical risk inputs
- Collaborate with Developers on implementation approaches and quality practices
- Advise the Project Manager on technical dependencies, sequencing, and estimates
- Work with the Product Manager and Stakeholders to explain technical trade-offs and options
- Coordinate with Security/Privacy, DevOps/Platform, and QA/Testing on applicable design and delivery requirements

## DevOps and Platform Engineers

### Role Summary
DevOps and Platform Engineers provide and operate the environments, infrastructure, and delivery capabilities needed to build, release, and monitor the product reliably.

### Responsibilities
- Support build and deployment pipelines, environments, and infrastructure
- Improve reliability, observability, and operational automation
- Identify platform and deployment risks and document operational requirements
- Help teams prepare and validate deployment and rollback approaches

### Key Deliverables and Coordination
- Provide pipeline and environment readiness, operational documentation, and reliability signals
- Work with Developers and the Technical Lead to integrate build, test, and deployment workflows
- Coordinate with QA/Testing on environment availability and release verification
- Partner with the Project Manager on infrastructure dependencies and delivery risks
- Coordinate with Customer Support or Operations representatives on handoff and operational readiness

## Business Analysts

### Role Summary
Business Analysts clarify business processes and requirements, helping stakeholders and delivery teams build a shared understanding of the problem and expected outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Elicit, analyze, and document business needs, workflows, and requirements
- Identify gaps, assumptions, and impacts across processes or stakeholder groups
- Help turn requirements into clear, testable acceptance criteria
- Support validation that delivered changes address the agreed needs

### Key Deliverables and Coordination
- Maintain requirements, process maps, decision records, or traceability as appropriate
- Work with the Product Manager and Stakeholders to clarify needs and resolve conflicting requirements
- Coordinate with Developers and QA/Testing so requirements and acceptance criteria are understood and verifiable
- Keep the Project Manager informed of scope questions, decisions, and dependencies

## Data and Analytics Leads

### Role Summary
Data and Analytics Leads help teams define how outcomes will be measured and ensure that the data used to assess results is meaningful and trustworthy.

### Responsibilities
- Translate product success measures into instrumentation and reporting needs
- Advise on event definitions, metric interpretation, and data quality
- Validate that measurement plans support the intended decisions
- Communicate results, limitations, and relevant trends to the team

### Key Deliverables and Coordination
- Define or review measurement plans, metric definitions, dashboards, and data-quality checks
- Partner with the Product Manager to align metrics with project outcomes
- Work with Developers on instrumentation and with QA/Testing on measurement validation
- Share findings with Stakeholders and the Project Manager to inform status, decisions, and follow-up work

## Security and Privacy Leads

### Role Summary
Security and Privacy Leads advise teams on security, privacy, and applicable compliance considerations and help ensure risks are assessed and addressed throughout delivery.

### Responsibilities
- Identify relevant security, privacy, and compliance requirements and risks
- Recommend appropriate controls and review plans for validating them
- Advise on data handling, access, threat scenarios, and risk mitigations as applicable
- Escalate security concerns through the established security incident and escalation paths

### Key Deliverables and Coordination
- Provide security/privacy requirements, risk-register inputs, and review or validation recommendations
- Work with Developers and the Technical Lead to incorporate safeguards into design and implementation
- Coordinate with QA/Testing on security and privacy validation where applicable
- Keep the Project Manager informed of risks, dependencies, and required decisions
- For security incidents, follow the security incident runbook and notify Security on-call; coordinate stakeholder communication through the established incident process

## Customer Support and Operations Representatives

### Role Summary
Customer Support and Operations Representatives bring customer-facing and operational perspectives to planning and delivery and help prepare teams to support changes after release.

### Responsibilities
- Share customer feedback, support trends, and operational constraints
- Identify support, training, documentation, and service-readiness needs
- Review known issues and help prepare appropriate customer or internal guidance
- Provide post-release feedback on adoption, incidents, and support needs

### Key Deliverables and Coordination
- Contribute support readiness plans, knowledge-base or operational content, and handoff requirements
- Work with the Product Manager to connect customer feedback to product priorities
- Coordinate with the Project Manager on release readiness, communications, and dependencies
- Work with Developers, QA/Testing, and DevOps/Platform Engineers on known issues, escalation routes, and support handoffs
- Provide relevant readiness information to Stakeholders before release and feedback after release

---

## How these personas are used in the exercise
- Use these persona definitions to frame scenarios and sample interactions in the Skills Exercise.
- Each persona can be used as a persona prompt for Copilot Spaces to shape role-specific guidance.
- A person may hold more than one role, but the team should make responsibility ownership and decision authority explicit.
