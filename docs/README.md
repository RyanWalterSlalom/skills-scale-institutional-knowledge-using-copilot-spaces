# OctoAcme Project Management Docs

A landing page for OctoAcme’s project management guidance. These documents describe a lightweight, cross-functional approach centered on customer value, iterative delivery, clear ownership, data-informed decisions, and continuous learning.

## Project management process summary

OctoAcme’s lifecycle moves through initiation, planning, execution, release, and closeout. Teams begin by validating the business need, identifying stakeholders, defining measurable outcomes, and deciding whether to proceed. Planning turns approved work into a prioritized backlog with acceptance criteria and a Definition of Done, while estimates, milestones, risks, and dependencies are made visible.

During execution, teams track work on a shared project board, deliver in small increments, and use pull requests, reviews, tests, and progress metrics to maintain quality and transparency. The Project Manager coordinates delivery, schedules, risks, and communications; the Product Manager owns outcomes, prioritization, and success measures; developers build and test, QA validates acceptance, and stakeholders provide input and approvals.

Communication includes regular team check-ins, delivery syncs and demos, and stakeholder updates. Risks and blockers are reviewed and escalated through the team, Project Manager, Product Lead, and sponsor as appropriate. Before release, teams verify acceptance criteria, CI and security checks, smoke tests, release notes, and rollback plans. After deployment, they verify and communicate results, then use retrospectives to track a small set of actionable improvements.
